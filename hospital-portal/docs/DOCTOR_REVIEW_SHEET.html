HealthTwin AI: doctor review sheet

Simulated prototype. Every medical statement here is sample content that has not been medically reviewed. For each line tick OK, tick Remove, or write the change under "Change to:". The portal keeps showing "Sample checklist. Pending medical review." until this signed sheet is checked and the reviewer name and date are entered in src/data/reviewStatus.js.

Generated by npm run review-sheet from src/data/. Press Print (Ctrl+P) to get the paper sheet.
